Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Chorushive ("we", "us", "our") is operated by RevOps Global LLC. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Data We Collect

When you connect your Spotify account, we receive and store:

  • Spotify user ID — to identify your account
  • Display name — to personalize your experience
  • Email address — to associate your account with a subscription
  • Subscription level (Free or Premium) — to determine feature access
  • Currently playing track — read in real time to display lyrics; never stored

We also collect standard server logs (IP address, browser type, access timestamps) for security and debugging purposes.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To authenticate you and provide the Chorushive service
  • To manage your subscription and process payments via Stripe
  • To display synchronized lyrics for your currently playing track
  • To send transactional emails (receipts, subscription notices)

We do not sell your data. We do not use your data for advertising.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Spotify — authentication and playback state. Governed by Spotify's Privacy Policy.
  • Stripe — payment processing for PRO subscriptions. Stripe stores your payment information; we do not. Governed by Stripe's Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase — secure database hosting for account data. Data is stored in the United States.
  • LRCLIB.NET — lyric data provider. No personal data is shared with LRCLIB.

4. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we remove your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law (e.g., payment records required for tax purposes, retained for 7 years).

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Disconnect Chorushive from Spotify at any time via your Spotify account settings

To exercise these rights, email us at hello@chorushive.com.

6. Cookies

We use session cookies to keep you logged in. We do not use third-party tracking cookies or analytics cookies.

7. Children's Privacy

Chorushive is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by email or by posting a notice on the site. Continued use of the service after changes constitutes acceptance.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email hello@chorushive.com.
