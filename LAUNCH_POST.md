LINKEDIN LAUNCH POST — Spotify Karaoke
---

Built something fun this weekend.

It's called Spotify Karaoke, and it does exactly what it sounds like: connect your Spotify, play a song, and watch the lyrics scroll word-by-word in real time — like proper karaoke, but for whatever's already in your queue.

A few things I'm proud of:
- Words highlight as they're sung, not just by line
- Your album art blurs out behind the lyrics (it actually looks great)
- It switches songs instantly, no refresh needed
- Zero cost to run — lyrics pulled from lrclib.net, no API key needed

It's live at: https://spotify-karaoke-nine.vercel.app

Stack: Node + Express, Spotify Web API, Vanilla JS, Vercel. The whole thing is maybe 300 lines of code.

I built this to scratch my own itch — I wanted something that worked on any song in my library without fiddling with a separate app. But honestly, it turned out polished enough to share.

Give it a try and let me know what you think. If your favorite song doesn't have synced lyrics, drop it in the comments — I'm curious where the coverage gaps are.
